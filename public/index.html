HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Snake Battle by Harshit Rawat

CONTROLS

PC: WASD (Player 1) / Arrow Keys (Player 2)

Mobile: On-screen D-pad

GAME MODES

Play Online — Auto-match with a random opponent (same mode)

vs AI — Battle the computer (Easy / Medium / Hard / Impossible)

Create Room — Private room with a 6-letter code to share

Join Room — Enter a friend's code. Supports up to 4 players!

Play Local — Two players on one device

SCORING

Each player starts with 30 points and length 3.

Eat food: +10 pts, +1 length

Bite self or opponent: -10 pts, -1 length to the bitten snake

Head-on collision: both snakes take damage

A snake is eliminated when score hits 0. Last snake wins. Simultaneous = DRAW.

SUDDEN DEATH

After 10 seconds, red walls begin closing in from all sides. Touch the walls and you take damage. The safe zone shrinks every 8 seconds. Food never spawns in the danger zone.

BLITZ MODE

All snakes start at normal speed. Every 10 seconds, movement speed increases for everyone. Up to 6 speed levels. Survive the chaos!

POWER-UPS

Colored glowing items appear on the board every ~15s. Eat them to gain a 10-second effect:

GHST

Invisibility — Pass through players and red walls (can't eat food)

SHD

Shield — Blocks the next hit entirely

2D

Double Damage — Your bites deal 20 pts instead of 10

2P

Double Points — Food gives you 20 pts instead of 10

SPD

Speed Boost — Move 2x faster for 10 seconds

KING OF THE CASTLE

A crown spawns at the center. First player to reach it picks it up!

Crowned player moves 1.5x fast. If any player runs into the King (head or body), the crown transfers.

Timer-based: 1:00 / 1:30 / 2:00. Winner = player who held the crown longest.

No food. No Double Damage/Points. Only Ghost and Speed Up power-ups (King consumes them on touch).

Available in: Online & Local modes only.

DETECTIVE SNAKE

One player is the Detective (blue hat). Others are Imposters disguised as NPC snakes.

Detective: Press E near a snake to inspect. Press F to scan (3 charges).

Imposters: Eat food for +1 pt. Use F to TELEPORT (2 charges) to escape! A flash will warn the detective.

Timer: 1 / 2 / 3 min. Detective wins if all caught. Highest-scoring imposter wins if time runs out.

TIPS

Use swipe controls on mobile in fullscreen mode for quick inputs.

Power-ups can turn the tide — pick them up strategically!

In Blitz mode, survival matters more than score as speed ramps up.

In King of the Castle, Ghost mode lets you pass through the crowned player without taking the crown.

Enjoy!
